Welcome to Seaty, the city built by YOUR comments. 🏙️

🏠 Comment on any video and you move in: I build you a house.
🏗️ Comment on each new video and your building grows +1 floor.
🏆 Every month, one citizen wins the Citizen Award. More floors = more chances.
🎖️ Active citizens earn medals in city challenges and stories.

Find your house in the live 3D city:
👉 https://pooria-heydarian.github.io/Seaty/

Let's build Seaty together. Subscribe and leave your first comment! 👇

CITIZEN AWARD: OFFICIAL RULES (summary)
• Organizer: the Seaty channel owner. YouTube is not a sponsor and is not involved in this award.
• Free to join. No purchase or payment needed. You must be 18+ (or the age of majority where you live).
• Entries: every floor you have is one entry (1 floor = 1 entry). You get 1 floor for each different Seaty video you comment on. Spam, bots and fake accounts are removed.
• Prize: 10% of the channel's YouTube income for the previous month. If the channel earned nothing that month, there is no cash prize.
• Draw: on the 1st of each month, using a public random number announced in advance. The full entry list and result are published so anyone can check it.
• The winner is announced in a video and a pinned comment, and has 14 days to reply. Otherwise a new winner is drawn.
• Void where prohibited. Taxes, if any, are the winner's responsibility.
